linear_gradient(direction(optional), color-1, color-2, ......., color-n)

You have to write atleast two color values.

direction: i) standard values
              to bottom (default)
              to right
              to top
              to left
              to top left
              to tot right
              to bottom right
              to bottom right
          ii) degree
              180 degree = to bottom
              0 degree = to top
              90 degree = to right
              270 degree = to left
              45 degree = to top right
              135 degree = to bottom right
              225 degree = to bottom left
              315 degree = to top left
          iii) turn
              1turn
              0.5turn
