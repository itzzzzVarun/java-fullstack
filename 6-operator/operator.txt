Operators are the symbol which is used to performed  some specific task.

=> There are seven types of Operators:-
    1> Arthmetic Operator (+, -, *, /...)
    2> Relational Operator (>, <, <=, >=, =)
    3> Comparision Operator (=, !=)
    4> Concat Operator (||)
    5> Logical Operator (AND, OR, NOT)
    6> Special Operator 
        i>   IN 
        ii>  NOT IN
        iii> between
        iv>  not between
        v>   is
        vi>  is not
        vii> like
        viii> not like 
    7> SubQUERY Operator
      i> all
      ii> any
      iii> exists
      iv> not exists
