Having clause is use to fitter the group.

syntax: select group by expression/group function from table_name [where <filter records condition>] 
        group by col_name/expression
        Having <filter group condition>

order of execution: 1. from
                    2. where (if used)
                    3. group by (row-by-row)
                    4. Having (group-by-group)
                    5. select (group-by-group)
